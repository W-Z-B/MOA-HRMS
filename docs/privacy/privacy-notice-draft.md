# Privacy notice for staff: starting draft

**Status:** a draft for GSA to complete and approve, not GSA's notice. Every item in square brackets is GSA's to
fill in or confirm, with its legal adviser and data protection officer. Once approved, the HR Manager publishes
it from the Admin screen (Privacy notice tab). Each member of staff then reads and acknowledges it at their next
sign-in, and the system records that they did (checklist item 1.31). Write it in plain words: it is read on a
phone, once, by everyone.

The headings follow the information the Data Protection Act 2023 requires a person to be given, as summarised in
the impact assessment (docs/privacy/dpia.md, section 5). The legal adviser should check them against the Act's
sections.

---

**How the Guyana School of Agriculture uses your personal data**

**Who we are.** The Guyana School of Agriculture Corporation, [address], decides how your personal data is used
in this system and is responsible for it. Our data protection officer is [name or post], [email], [phone].

**What we hold.** Your staff record: your name, date of birth, contact details, the people to call in an
emergency, your dependants, your qualifications and work before GSA, your appointment and contract, your leave,
your national ID, NIS number and TIN, and the bank account your pay goes to. If you send a doctor's note with a
sick-leave request, we hold that too. The system also records when you sign in and from which device.

**Why.** To employ you and pay you: your appointment, contract and leave, and the returns the law requires to
the National Insurance Scheme and the Guyana Revenue Authority. [Add any other purpose GSA has, such as staff
development, and say whether giving each item is required by law or by your contract.]

**Who sees it.** You see your whole record under My record. Human Resources see the records of the staff they
look after, and your manager sees what deciding your leave needs. Finance sees what your pay needs. Doctor's
notes are seen by Human Resources only. The auditor may read the record of what was done in the system. [Name
anyone outside GSA who receives your data, for example NIS, GRA, the bank that pays you, and the Ministry of
Agriculture, which receives totals, not records of individuals.] Every look at your identity numbers or bank
details is recorded.

**Where it is kept.** On [GSA's own server / a provider's server] in Guyana. [Confirm. If any of it goes outside
Guyana, for example email, say where and what protects it.]

**How long.** [For each kind of record, the period in GSA's retention schedule, for example: your staff record
for [the period] after you leave; doctor's notes for two years after the leave they support.] Then it is deleted.

**Your rights.** You can:

- see everything we hold about you, and keep a copy (My record, Download as a file);
- ask us to correct anything that is wrong (My record, Something wrong?). Human Resources answer within
  [30] days and tell you what they did, or why they did not change it;
- [ask us to stop using part of your data while a correction is looked at, or to object to a use: write to the
  data protection officer];
- [ask us to delete data we no longer need];
- complain to the Data Protection Commissioner, [contact details].

**Questions.** Ask Human Resources, or write to the data protection officer at [email].
